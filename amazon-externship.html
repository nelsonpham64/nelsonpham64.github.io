Amazon Workforce Sentiment & Burnout Analysis

Amazon Externship Project

Analyzing employee feedback from Glassdoor and YouTube to identify burnout risks and deliver actionable workforce recommendations

Python pandas NLTK TextBlob Matplotlib Google Colab Excel

Problem Statement

Amazon fulfillment center employees were experiencing significant burnout and exhaustion, but the volume of qualitative feedback made manual review impractical. This project aimed to answer: What are the root causes of burnout among fulfillment center employees, and which interventions would have the greatest impact on morale and retention?

💼 Glassdoor Reviews

  • Format: Structured pros, cons, and advice fields
  • Tone: Mostly positive or neutral — balanced and professional
  • Who's writing: Employees formally sharing workplace experiences
  • Strength: Easy to analyze due to clear, consistent structure

▶️ YouTube Comments & Transcripts

  • Format: Free-form, unstructured text
  • Tone: Predominantly negative and emotionally charged
  • Who's writing: Public audience reacting to Amazon workplace videos
  • Strength: Revealed frustrations that formal reviews tended to soften

Methodology

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

Built an end-to-end text preprocessing pipeline in Google Colab to prepare both datasets for analysis. Raw text from Glassdoor reviews and YouTube transcripts was cleaned and standardized into a consistent, analysis-ready format.

Key Preprocessing Steps

  • Loaded raw CSVs into pandas DataFrames and inspected for nulls and duplicates
  • Converted all text to lowercase and stripped punctuation
  • Removed English stopwords using NLTK to reduce noise
  • Combined Glassdoor's pros, cons, advice, and summary fields into a single cleaned text column
  • Exported cleaned datasets as new CSVs for downstream analysis

Keyword & Sentiment Analysis

After cleaning, keyword frequency analysis was performed on both datasets to surface the most common themes. Sentiment classification using TextBlob was applied to categorize feedback as positive, neutral, or negative — enabling a structured comparison between the two platforms.

Analysis Techniques

  • Word frequency counting using Python's Counter to find top unigrams
  • Noise word filtering to remove generic terms (e.g. "get", "amazon", "job")
  • Bar chart visualization of top 10 keywords per platform using Matplotlib
  • Sentiment classification to compare tone across Glassdoor vs. YouTube

Key Findings

The analysis revealed a clear and consistent burnout signal across both platforms:

  • Burnout was the #1 theme — emotionally charged negative feedback consistently pointed to exhaustion as the core problem
  • Full-time associates were hit hardest — working the longest hours, facing cumulative physical fatigue, and being the most expensive segment to lose to turnover
  • Burnout drives a chain reaction: burnout → lower morale → higher turnover → lower productivity
  • Production targets were set by algorithms optimized for speed, with no adjustment for task difficulty or experience level
  • Break culture undermined recovery — workload pressure made employees feel they couldn't afford to take their breaks
  • YouTube revealed what Glassdoor softened — the platform difference itself was a finding, showing a gap between formal reporting and real employee sentiment

Recommendation

Based on the analysis, the top recommendation was a low-cost, measurable pilot program:

Pilot: Task Rotation + Flexible Pacing

  • What: Team leads rotate associates between tasks and temporarily adjust pacing during high-fatigue periods
  • Where: One zone with full-time associates
  • When: 2-week pilot during high-volume shifts
  • Owner: Learning Ambassador / Floor Team Lead
  • Success metrics: End-of-shift fatigue feedback, number of rotations used, reduction in strain complaints
